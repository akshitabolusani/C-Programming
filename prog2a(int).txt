int   datatype
-----------------
1) How  much  memory  space  does  int  take  ?  --->  4B  i.e.  32  bits
								                     Eg: 1010  0100  1100  0110  1101  0001  1111   0001

2) What  is  the  first  bit  called  ?  --->  Sign  bit
     What  is  the  length  of  magnitude ?  --->  31  bits

3) What  is  the  maximum  value  of  integer ?  --->  2 ^ 31 - 1
     What  is  the  minimum  value  of  integer ?  --->  -2 ^ 31 
     
4) Why  is  int  called  an  extension  of  short ?  ---> Since  short  is  2B  and  int  is  4B

5) What  are  the  macros  for  maximum  and  minimum  values  of  integer ?  --->  INT_MAX  and  INT_MIN  

6) Where  are  INT_MAX  and  INT_MIN  defined ?  --->  In  <limits.h>  file

7) What  is  the  format  for  integer ?  --->  %d  (or) %i

8) What  is  the  most  frequently  used  data  type  in  'c'  language ?  --->  int 

9) integer  x;
    Is  the  above  statement  valid ?  --->  No  becoz  there  is  no  integer  datatype

10) Int  x;
      Is  the  above  statement  valid ?  --->  No  becoz  int  is  a  keyword  and  hence  it  should  be  in  lowercase
