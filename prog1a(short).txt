short  datatype
-------------------
1) How  much  memory  space  does  short  take ?  --->  2B  i.e.  16 bits
											 Eg:  1010  0101  1110  0111

2) What  is  the  first  bit  called ?  --->  Sign  bit
    What  about  remaining  15  bits ? ---> Magnitude  bits

3) What  does  signbit = 0  indicate  ? --->  Positive  magnitude
    What  does  signbit = 1  indicate ? --->   Negative  magnitude

4) Is  0010  0101  1110  0111  a  positive  (or)  negative  number  ?  --->  Positive  becoz  sign  bit  is  0
    What  about   1010  0101  1110  0110  ?  --->  Negative  becoz  sign  bit  is  1

5) What  is  the  maximum  value  of  short  integer ?  --->  2 ^ 15 - 1 = 32767
     What  is  the  minimum  value  of  short  integer ?  --->  -2 ^ 15 = -32768
     	     
6) What  are  the  macros  for  maximum  and  minimum  values  of  short  integer ?  ---> 	SHRT_MAX   and  SHRT_MIN  

7) Where  are  SHRT_MAX  and  SHRT_MIN  defined ?  --->  In  <limits.h>  file

8) What  is  the  format  for  short  integer ?  --->  %hd  (or)  %hi  

9) short  int   x;
    short  x;
    Are  the  above  two  statements  same ?  --->  Yes  becoz  int  is  optional  in  short  int

10) Short  x;
      Is  the  above  statement  valid ?  --->   No  becoz  short  is   a  keyword  and  hence  it  should  be  in  lowercase
