long  long  datatype
------------------------
1) How  much  memory  space  does  long  long  take  ?  --->  8B  i.e.  64  bits

2) What  is  the  maximum  value  of  long  long  integer ?  --->  2 ^ 63 - 1
     What  is  the  minimum  value  of  long  long  integer ?  --->  -2 ^ 63
     
3) Why  is  long  long  called  an  extension  of  long ?  --->  Since  long  is  4B  and  long   long  is  8B

4) What  is  25LL  called ?  --->  long  long  integer  due  to  LL
     What  is  25L  called ?  --->   long  integer  due  to  L
     What  is  25  called ?  --->   Integer  becoz  there  is  no  L

5) How  are  long  and  long  long  distinguished  ?  --->  LL  (or)  ll  for  long  long  and  L  for  long

6) What  are  the  macros  for  maximum  and  minimum  values  of  long  long  integer ?  --->  LONG_LONG_MAX  and  LONG_LONG_MIN  

7) Where  are  they  defined  ?  --->  In  <limits.h>  file

8) What  is  the  format  for  long  long  integer ?  --->  %lld  (or)  %lli

9) long  long  int  x;
    long  long  x;  
    Are  the  above  two  statements  same ?  --->  Yes  becoz  int  is  optional  in  long  long  int

10) Long  Long  x;
      Is  the  above  statement  valid ?  --->  No  becoz  long  long  is  a  keyword  and  hence  it  should  be  in  lowercase
