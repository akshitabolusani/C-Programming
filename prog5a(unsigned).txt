unsigned   datatype
------------------------
1) What  is  the  size  of  unsigned  integer ?  --->  4B  i.e.  32  bits
									         Eg: 1010  0100  1100  0110  1101  0001  1111   0001

2) What  is  the  length  of  magnitude ?  --->  32  bits  becoz  there  is  no  sign  bit

3) Why  is  there  no  sign  bit  ?  --->  Since  it  is  unsigned

4) What  is  the  maximum  value  of  unsigned  integer ?  --->  2 ^ 32 - 1
     What  is  the  minimum  value  of  unsigned  integer ?  --->  0
     
5) Why  are  negative  numbers  not  permitted  ?  --->  Since  they  are  signed numbers  due  to  -  sign

6) How  is  unsigned  different  from  remaining  datatypes ?  --->  
		Both  +ve  and  -ve  values  are  permitted  with  other  datatypes  but  unsigned  supports  only  +ve  values

7) What  is  the  macro  for  maximum  value  of  unsigned  integer ?  --->  UINT_MAX  
    What  is  the  macro  for  minimum  value  of  unsigned  integer ?  --->  No  macro  becoz  min  value  is  0

8) Where  is  UINT_MAX  defined ?  --->  In  <limits.h>  file

9) What  is  the  format  for  unsigned  integer ?  --->  %u  but  not  %ud  (or)  %ui

10) What  is  the  suffix  of  unsigned  number ?  --->  U  (or)  u

11) What  is  25U  called ?  --->  unsigned  integer

12) unsigned  int  x;
      unsigned  x;
     Are  the  above  two  statements  same ?  --->  Yes  becoz  int  is  optional  in  unsigned  int

13) Unsigned  x;
      Is  the  above  statement  valid ?  --->  No  becoz  unsigned  is  a  keyword  and  hence  it  should  be  in  lowercase  


Note:
Positive  numbers  are  called  unsigned  numbers  becoz  there  is  no  sign  and  
negative  numbers  are  called  signed  numbers  due  to  -  sign
