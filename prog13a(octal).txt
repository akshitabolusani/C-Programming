Octal  integer
----------------
1) What  is  the  prefix  of  octal  number ?  --->  0

2) Which  digits  are  permitted  in  octal  number ? --->  0  to  7

3) What  is  the  base  of  octal  number ?  --->  8  due  to  eight  digits  0 to  7

4) Which  digits  are  not  permitted  in  octal  number ?  --->  8  and  9

5) What  is  the  format  for  octal  number ? --->  %o  but  not  %O
 
6) Which  of  the  following  are  valid  octal  numbers ?
     a) 09248  --->  Invalid  due  to  9  and  8
     b) 06247  --->  Valid
     c) 2315  ---> Invalid  becoz  there  is  no  prefix  0
     d) 0X1234  ---> Invalid  due  to  'X'
     e) 0B10101  ---> Invalid  due  to  'B'
     f) 062C47  --->  Invalid  due  to  'C'

7) octal  int  x  = 06247  
     Is  the  above  statement  valid ?  --->  No  becoz  there  is  no  octal  keyword
