Variable  Initialization
---------------------------
1) int  x = 25;
    What  is  the  name  of  variable ?  --->  'x' 
    What  is  the  value  of  variable ?  --->  25       
    What  is  the  size  of  variable ?  --->  4B
    What  is  the  address  of  variable ?  ---> &x 

2) What  is   '&'  operator  called  ?  --->  Address  operator

3) How  to  obtain  address  of  a  variable ?  --->  &variable

4) What  does  &x  do ?  --->  Gives  address  of  variable  'x'  (may  be  1000)  

5) int  k;
    What  is  the  value  of  variable  'k' ?  ---> Garbage  value  becoz  it  is  not  initialized

6) float  m = 10.8;
    What  is  the  name  of  variable ?  --->  m 
    What  is  the  value  of  variable ?  --->  10.8
    What  is  the  size  of  variable ?  --->  4B
    What  is  the  address  of  variable ?  ---> &m
    
7) char  ch = 'g';
    What  is  the  name  of  variable ?  --->  ch
    What  is  the  value  of  variable ?  --->  'g'
    What  is  the  size  of  variable ?  --->  1B
    What  is  the  address  of  variable ?  --->  &ch
